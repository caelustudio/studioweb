### Reporting a Vulnerability

We take the security of this project seriously. If you discover a security vulnerability, we appreciate your responsible disclosure.

### How to Report

Please report security vulnerabilities by caelustudio@outlook.com.

Include the following information in your report:

A clear description of the vulnerability and its potential impact
Step-by-step instructions to reproduce the issue (proof-of-concept is welcome)
The affected version(s) you have identified
Any suggestions for mitigation or fixes, if available
What to Expect


### Acknowledgment​

We will acknowledge receipt of your report within 15 day


##### Initial Assessment​

We will provide an initial assessment and triage result within 5 business days​


##### Status Updates​

You will receive progress updates at least every 7 days​ until the issue is resolved


##### Resolution​

A fix or mitigation will be released as soon as possible, depending on complexity


Outcome
If the vulnerability is accepted:​ We will work on a patch and coordinate a release. You will be credited in the security advisory (unless you prefer to remain anonymous). We may also invite you to review the fix before public disclosure.
If the vulnerability is declined:​ We will provide a detailed explanation of why the report does not meet our criteria for a security issue, and we welcome further discussion if you believe the assessment is incorrect.
Responsible Disclosure:​ We kindly ask that you do not publicly disclose the vulnerability until we have released a patch and given users reasonable time to upgrade.

Thank you for helping keep this project and its users safe!
